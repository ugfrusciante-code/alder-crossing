# Reference cross-check · October 5, 2026

The art direction is grounded painterly medieval 3D. The production-kit sheet is the closer implementation target; the panoramic concept establishes atmosphere and composition.

| Reference requirement | Revised implementation | Remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timber, limewash, straw and stone form a consistent family | Shared generated material atlas, matte surfaces, steeper layered roofs, timber braces, shutters, stone foundation courses and dormers | Meshes are still procedural; silhouette and weathering variations need bespoke asset work |
| Organic village within a landscape | Curved paths with broken/feathered edges, soil around houses, kitchen gardens, woven fences, a well, cart and village trees | Opening settlement is sparse compared with the mature village painting |
| Layered woodland silhouettes | Generated alpha foliage on intersecting cards, branching trunks, varied sizes and colors; trees clear around new construction | Species differentiation, richer trunks and authored shrubs remain limited |
| Warm light, cool water, restrained color | Front-facing warm key light, sky ambient/reflection, textured meadow, animated river surface | Atmosphere, banks and water reflections remain simpler than the painting |
| Readable unobtrusive interface | Slate/parchment palette, serif names, labeled tools, six headline indicators | Some dense economic information and larger text scaling need further refinement |
| Direct navigation | Drag pan, pointer-centered zoom, visible rotate/home controls, building mesh selection, guided food category | Formation dragging/facing and a fuller onboarding sequence are not implemented |

The first revision's uniform block trees, hard strip roads and repeated UI recreation were clear misses. This revision replaces those elements and checks the live scene. Do not describe the final art as a perfect match or finished AAA fidelity.
