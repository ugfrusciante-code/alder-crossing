# Alder Crossing 0.3 — 7 October 2026

Building placement now supports quarter turns. The entrance marker, occupied footprint, worker routes, crops and surrounding clearance follow the chosen orientation. Cancel unfinished construction from its inspector to recover reserved materials and release builders.

Production buildings accept a granary stock target. Stored goods, cargo and batches already underway count toward this target; existing batches finish, so a batch may slightly exceed the number. Production resumes when the reserve drops. An empty target means continuous production.

The economy ledger distinguishes stored, carried and construction-reserved goods. Winter planning shows the food and household firewood needed for the current population and housing. It is a baseline rather than a survival forecast: future production, spoilage, workshop fuel and outpost meals are additional.

The standalone Mac application includes its assets and runs offline. Desktop and browser saves are separate; Export / Import transfers them. Existing version-2 save files remain supported.

Validation: ten simulation scenarios and the complete legal charter playthrough pass. Interactive checks covered restoring an existing save, placing a rotated windmill, editing its stock target, cancelling it, and confirming the original material balances in the ledger. The packaged Mac app was launched from its local offline protocol, its charter started, and its rendered game and pause controls checked. Its ad-hoc signature passed strict verification.

The reference art remains the target. The current game still needs bespoke architecture, denser settlement composition and a stronger lighting pass to reach that fidelity.
